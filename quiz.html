Did You Know?

Fascinating facts about global food production and security

  • Global Production Ranking

    China is the world’s largest food producer, followed by India, the United States, Brazil, and Russia.

    Brazil accounts for 7% of global food production and is the largest exporter of coffee, soybeans, and orange juice.

  • Brazil: Production and Hunger

    Despite being one of the world’s largest food producers, Brazil has 8,7 million people facing food insecurity.

    Unequal income distribution and the lack of effective public policies are the main causes.

  • Hydroponic Efficiency

    Hydroponics can produce 10 times more food in the same space than traditional agriculture.

    It also saves up to 90% of water and can be practiced in urban environments.

  • Zero Waste

    If we eliminated all food waste, we could feed an additional 2 billion people.

    About one third of all food produced worldwide is lost or wasted.
